IRON OATH

— T A C T I C S —

Lead the Iron Oath mercenary company across ten story missions to break Warlord Vael's grip on Elderreach. An original grid-based, turn-based fantasy tactics RPG made in Godot 4.

▶ Play in browser
Free · no install · mouse, keyboard or touch · progress saves automatically

Features

Isometric grid with height

Hills, forests, marsh, rivers, forts and thrones. High ground hits harder and gives archers +1 range.

Weapon triangle

Swords beat axes, axes beat lances, lances beat swords. Magic targets resistance instead of armor.

Facing & backstabs

Every unit faces a direction. Flank for +20%, strike from behind for +50% and bonus crit.

Smart enemy AI

Foes focus wounded and fragile units, avoid your threat zones, seek cover and gang up on healers.

10-mission campaign

Rout, Defend, Escort and Seize objectives with reinforcements, bosses and a short story between battles.

Classic or Casual

Optional permadeath. Level up, promote at level 8 with an Oath Crest, buy gear and hire mercenaries.

Eight classes

Knight → PaladinSword · Shield Bash, Bulwark
Archer → RangerBow · Aimed Shot, Arrow Rain
Mage → ArchmageTome · Fireball, Frost Lance

Controls

Select / moveTap or click a blue unit, then a blue tile
ActAttack, ability or Wait from the action bar; tap a target to preview, tap again to confirm
InspectTap an enemy to see its threat range; hover or tap tiles for terrain bonuses
CameraDrag to pan, mouse wheel or +/- to zoom
KeysEsc cancel / pause · E end turn
